    What we set up for your event

    • A registration page in your colours and languages, with the questions your event needs.
    • A confirmation for each guest, with their badge and the practical details.
    • The list of who is coming, up to date at any time.
    • Badges printed in advance or on site, a colour for each type of guest.
    • Check-in at the door by our hostesses, and access to each room or area controlled.
    • The attendance list, sent to you after the event.
