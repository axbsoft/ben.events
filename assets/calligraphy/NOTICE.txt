benevents agency — calligraphy experience (Chinese, Japanese, Korean)

The .json files of this folder hold the outlines of the characters and letters the pages draw.
They come from open-source typefaces, each under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org):

  Ma Shan Zheng      Chinese brush script
                     Copyright 2018 The MaShanZheng Project Authors
                     https://github.com/googlefonts/mashanzheng
  Yuji Boku          Japanese brush script (the katakana, in their forms for writing downwards)
                     Copyright 2021 The Yuji Project Authors
                     https://github.com/Kinutafontfactory/Yuji
  East Sea Dokdo     Korean brush script (enlarged to the size of the other two)
                     Copyright (c) YoonDesign Inc.
                     https://fonts.google.com/specimen/East+Sea+Dokdo
  Instrument Serif   the visitor's own name, in Latin letters
                     Copyright 2022 The Instrument Serif Project Authors
                     https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif

In templates/: the EXEED bookmark is the picture supplied for that project, unchanged; the three
plain bookmarks (hanzi, kana, hangul) are drawn by tools/calligraphy/make_template.js.
The name lists and the syllable table are in tools/calligraphy/.
