Events · Open day

Open days in showrooms and dealerships

A showroom dressed from morning to evening: the product on a lit stage, a welcome bar, a DJ and a game, then a different light after dark.

The service:Brand & Corporate Receptions

Talk to us about your event

An open day brings people who were passing by into your showroom, and gives those you invited a reason to stay. It runs for a whole day, so it needs a rhythm: a welcome that catches the eye from the street, something to do once inside, and an evening that feels different from the morning.

For CUPRA, we dressed the showroom from morning to evening. The Formentor stood on a black stage with a green glow, totems in the brand’s colours stood inside and out on the street, and a DJ booth with a backlit logo played through the day. A welcome bar and dressed high tables received the visitors, a “Precision Challenge” game kept them around, and after dark the showroom turned green and blue.

We take care of the set, the stage, the lighting, the sound, the DJ set-up and the games.

What we take care of

  • The product on a lit stage or podium
  • Totems and signage, inside and on the street
  • DJ booth, sound and moving lights
  • Welcome bar and dressed high tables
  • Games and activities for visitors
  • Evening lighting of the showroom

An open day, hour by hour

The same showroom, three moments.

  • MorningThe street side dressed, the product lit, the welcome bar open for the first visitors.
  • AfternoonThe DJ, the game and the sales team at work among the visitors.
  • EveningThe light changes, the music rises, invited guests arrive for the last part of the day.

Frequent questions

Do you organise open days in showrooms and dealerships?

Yes. For CUPRA, we dressed the showroom from morning to evening: the car on a lit stage, totems in the brand’s colours, a welcome bar, a DJ, the lighting of the evening and a game for the visitors.

Can you plan something for visitors to do?

Yes. For CUPRA, a “Precision Challenge” game kept visitors around the stand, next to the DJ booth and the welcome bar.

Can you dress the street side too?

Yes. For CUPRA, totems in the brand’s colours stood inside the showroom and out on the street.

When do we know the budget?

Before anything is booked. The concept and a clear budget are presented to you together.

An event like this in mind?

Give us the date, the place and the number of guests: we will come back with first ideas.
