Events · Seminar

Corporate seminars in Morocco

Plenary sessions, workshops and an evening that brings the team together: a seminar designed around what you want your people to take home.

The service:Conferences & Conventions

Talk to us about your event

A seminar is a few days that should change how a team works when it is back at the office. Everything we design follows from that: the order of the sessions, the layout of the room, the moments when people talk to each other rather than listen, and the evening that closes the day.

For MTA’s cycle meeting at the Fairmont Tazi Palace in Tangier, the plenary ran on a stage with a centre LED wall by day, and the same room became a gala dinner in front of a wall of LED artwork by night. That is the kind of seminar we like to produce: one place, several moments, no dead time between them.

We take care of the stage, the screens, the sound, the light and the control desk with our own team, and we coordinate the venue and the other suppliers. You deal with one producer, from the first brief to the last session.

What we take care of

  • Format and running order of the days
  • Choice of the venue and of the rooms
  • Plenary stage, LED screens and room dressing
  • Sound, light and video control
  • The evening: dinner, gala or cocktail
  • Filming and live direction

How we run a seminar

Four steps, with the same producer at each of them.

  • ListenYour objective, your teams, the dates and the city: what the seminar must achieve.
  • DesignThe format, the venue, the stage and a clear budget, presented before anything is booked.
  • PrepareRunning order, content for the screens, technical plan and rehearsal with the speakers.
  • RunOn the day, our team runs every session and every change of room from the control desk.

Frequent questions

In which cities do you organise seminars?

In Casablanca, Rabat and Marrakech, and wherever in Morocco your seminar takes place. We produced MTA’s cycle meeting at the Fairmont Tazi Palace in Tangier.

Can the same room host the sessions and the dinner?

Yes. For MTA, the plenary room was turned into a gala dinner for the evening, in front of a wall of LED artwork.

Who is our contact while the seminar is being prepared?

One dedicated producer, from the first conversation to the last session. They coordinate the venue, the suppliers, the technical side and the schedule.

When do we know the budget?

Before anything is booked. The format, the venue and a clear budget are presented to you together.
