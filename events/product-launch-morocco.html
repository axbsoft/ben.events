Events · Product launch

Product launches and brand events

A product on a lit stage, a set in your colours, guests who leave with one image in mind: launches and brand evenings designed around what you present.

The service:Brand & Corporate Receptions

Talk to us about your event

A launch has one job: to make a product, or a brand, the centre of attention for an evening. We start from what you present and from the people you invite, clients, partners, the press, and we build the place around it: where the product stands, how it is lit, what guests see when they walk in.

For Volvo’s 70th anniversary, “Built on Trust. Driving Morocco Forward.”, an industrial hall was dressed with an LED screen and cocktail tables made of oil drums in the brand’s colours. For CUPRA, the Formentor stood on a black stage with a green glow, with totems in the brand’s colours inside the showroom and out on the street.

Stage, screens, set, lighting, sound and the running order of the presentation are designed together and run by our own team.

What we take care of

  • Concept of the evening and of the reveal
  • Set and stage in your colours
  • Lighting of the product and of the room
  • LED screens and content
  • Sound, speeches and music
  • Welcome of guests and the press

What makes a launch work

Four questions we answer before drawing anything.

  • What is shownThe product, the brand, or both: the centre of the room and of the evening.
  • Who is invitedClients, partners, dealers, the press: each expects something different.
  • The revealThe moment the product appears: light, sound and screens timed together.
  • What remainsPhotos, a film, an image that guests share: planned from the start.

Frequent questions

Can you organise a launch in our showroom?

Yes. We have dressed showrooms for CUPRA and for the Centrale Automobile Chérifienne: stage or podium, screens, lighting and high tables.

Can the set be in our brand colours?

Yes. Stage, totems, lighting and screen content are designed in your colours, as for Volvo and CUPRA.

Can you film the launch?

Yes. Filming and live direction are handled by our own team, from our control desk.

When do we know the budget?

Before anything is booked. The concept, the set and a clear budget are presented to you together.

An event like this in mind?

Give us the date, the place and the number of guests: we will come back with first ideas.
