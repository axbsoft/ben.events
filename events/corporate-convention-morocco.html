Events · Convention

Corporate conventions in Morocco

Your whole network in one room, one message on stage: a convention staged so that every speaker is heard and the evening is remembered.

The service:Conferences & Conventions

Talk to us about your event

A convention is the day a company speaks to all of its people at once: its sales force, its agents, its distributors. The message has to land in the back row as clearly as in the front, and the day has to move from one speaker to the next without a gap.

For RMA, under the banner “Ensemble, construisons l’avenir”, we built a circular centre stage with a runway to a curved LED wall, and dinner tables all around it, so that the speakers were close to everyone. For LEO’s regional distributor conference, delegates sat in classroom layout facing a triple LED screen. The room follows the message, not the other way round.

Stage, screens, screen content, sound, light, filming and the dinner that ends the day: all of it is designed together and run from our own control desk.

What we take care of

  • Concept and running order of the day
  • Stage design: centre stage, runway, LED walls
  • Content for the screens and speaker support
  • Sound, light and video control
  • Filming, live direction and broadcast
  • The dinner and the evening programme

How we stage a convention

The message first, then the room around it.

  • The messageWhat the audience must leave with, and who says it, in what order.
  • The roomStage, screens and seating drawn so that everyone sees and hears.
  • The rehearsalEvery speaker on stage before the day, with their slides and their microphone.
  • The dayOne control desk for sound, light and video, and one producer beside you.

Frequent questions

Can the convention be filmed and broadcast?

Yes. Multi-camera filming and live direction are handled by our own team, from our control desk, for the room as well as for the broadcast.

Can the speakers rehearse on stage?

Yes. We plan a rehearsal with each speaker, their slides and their microphone, before the guests arrive.

Can the dinner take place in the same room?

Yes. At RMA’s convention the dinner tables were set all around the stage, and for MTA we turned the plenary room into a gala dinner for the evening.

When do we know the budget?

Before anything is booked. The concept, the stage design and a clear budget are presented to you together.
