Gala evenings in Morocco

Dressed tables, a stage, light that changes with the evening: a gala dinner designed as a show, from the welcome to the last song.

The service:Ceremonies & Gala Evenings

Talk to us about your event

A gala evening is remembered for its atmosphere: the moment guests walk in, the light on the tables, the stage when the speeches start, the music when they end. We design it as a sequence, scene by scene, and we run every change of light and sound from our control desk.

At MTA’s cycle meeting in Tangier, dinner was served in front of a wall of LED artwork, in the room that had hosted the plenary sessions that same day. For RMA, the dinner tables were set all around a circular stage. In Marrakech, a cocktail evening took place under a canopy of golden lanterns, with a live DJ set and warm amber light.

We take care of the stage, the screens, the lighting, the sound, the table dressing and the running order of the night, and we coordinate the venue and its suppliers so that the evening runs without a pause.

What we take care of

  • Concept and running order of the evening
  • Stage, LED walls and screen content
  • Lighting design: tables, stage, room
  • Sound for the speeches and the music
  • Table dressing and room layout
  • Artists and live entertainment, coordinated

An evening in four scenes

Each scene has its light, its sound and its moment in the running order.

  • The welcomeThe entrance, the first drink, the light that tells guests the evening has started.
  • The dinnerDressed tables, soft light and music low enough to talk.
  • The stageSpeeches, awards or a show, with the light and the screens behind them.
  • The partyThe tables cleared, the music up, the light changed for the end of the night.

Frequent questions

Can the gala dinner follow a day of sessions in the same room?

Yes. For MTA in Tangier, we turned the plenary room into a gala dinner in front of a wall of LED artwork.

Do you organise evenings outdoors, in a garden or a riad?

Yes. An outdoor setting needs its own power supply, respect for sound limits and a plan B for wind or rain: we plan all three from the start.

Who runs the evening on the night?

Our team, from our control desk: sound, light and screens follow the running order, scene by scene, with your producer beside you.

When do we know the budget?

Before anything is booked. The concept, the mood boards and a clear budget are presented to you together.
