The agency

The agency that designs your event and builds it.

benevents agency is an event design and production agency. We bring concept, scenography and audiovisual production together under one roof, so that every element, from the stage to the screen content, speaks with a single voice.

Talk to us about your event

Why one team

Companies, institutions and foundations trust us with the moments that matter to them. Our promise is simple: an event that looks effortless on the day, because nothing was left to chance beforehand.

Design and production are often two jobs, given to two companies. We keep them in the same hands. The team that draws your stage is the one that lights it, fills its screens and runs it from the control desk on the day: fewer go-betweens, faster decisions, and no gap between the sketch and the room.

Each project has one dedicated producer, your contact from the first conversation to the final toast.
