What we produce · Hostesses & registration

Hostesses, registration and badges

From the invitation to the seat: guests registered, welcomed, given their badge and shown the way by a team dressed for your event.

Talk to us about your event

Guests judge an event in its first minutes: how they were invited, how quickly they get in, whether someone knows their name. A smooth welcome puts them in the right mood for everything that follows, and gives you an exact picture of who came.

We take care of the whole path of your guests: the registration form and the confirmations, the badges, the welcome desk and the hostesses, the check-in at the door, and the people who take speakers and VIP guests to their seat. After the event, you receive the list of who came.

What we provide

01

Hostesses and hosts

A team briefed on your event and dressed to match it, at the entrance, in the rooms and backstage.

02

Welcome desk

A desk in your colours, where guests are greeted, checked in and given what they need for the day.

03

Online registration

A registration form for your guests, confirmation messages, and an up-to-date list of who is coming.

04

Badges and lanyards

Badges printed in advance or on site, with names, companies and a colour for each type of guest.

05

Check-in and access control

Guests checked in at the door, access to each room or area controlled, and the attendance list sent to you after the event.

06

Speakers and VIP guests

Someone to meet each speaker and VIP guest, and take them to their seat and to the stage on time.

The path of your guests

Four steps, from the first invitation to the last guest.

  • ListWho is invited, how they register, and what each type of guest needs.
  • PrepareRegistration form, badges and the briefing of the team, ready before the day.
  • WelcomeDesk, hostesses and signs in place before the first guest arrives.
  • ReportWho came, sent to you after the event.
