What we produce · Rental & fit-out

Event rental and fit-out

Furniture, tents, equipment, computers and decoration: everything a venue needs to be ready, delivered, set up and collected.

Talk to us about your event

An empty hall, a garden or a showroom becomes an event venue with the right furniture, a roof when the weather calls for one, power, screens, and the details that make guests feel looked after. We bring all of it, so that you deal with one supplier instead of five.

Everything is chosen to match the look of the event, delivered and set up before your guests arrive, and collected afterwards. On the day, the same team that fitted out the venue is there to run it.

How we fit out a venue

Four steps, with one team from the visit to the collection.

  • VisitWe see the venue, or work from its plans: access, power, sizes and timing.
  • ChooseFurniture, décor and equipment proposed together, in the look of the event, with a clear budget.
  • Set upDelivered and installed before the guests arrive, and checked with you on site.
  • CollectTaken down and collected after the event, leaving the venue as we found it.
