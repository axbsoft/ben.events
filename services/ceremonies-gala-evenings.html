Ceremonies & Gala Evenings

Award and graduation ceremonies, gala dinners and anniversary evenings, with show direction and live entertainment.

Talk to us about your event

A ceremony happens only once. Awards, graduation, gala dinner or anniversary evening: we write the storyline, set every entrance on stage and hold show control, so that the emotion arrives at the right moment.

Décor, lighting, seating plan, host, artists and entertainment come together under one show direction. On the day, our team coordinates every sequence, from the welcome of the guests to the last toast.

What we take care of

  • Storyline and running order
  • Staging of awards and diplomas
  • Décor, lighting and seating plan
  • Host, artists and entertainment
  • Show control and coordination on the day

Frequent questions

Do you write the running order of the ceremony?

Yes. We write the storyline, set every entrance on stage and hold show control on the day, so that each sequence arrives at the right moment.

Do you take care of the host and the artists?

Yes. Host, artists and entertainment come together under one show direction.

Can one room hold the plenary and then the gala dinner?

Yes. We did it in Tangier: plenary sessions on stage by day, then the gala dinner in the same room in the evening, in front of a wall of LED artwork.

Do you also work for institutions and foundations?

Yes. Companies, institutions and foundations trust us with their ceremonies: graduations, closing ceremonies, awards.

An event like this in mind?

Give us the date, the place and the number of guests: we will come back with first ideas.
