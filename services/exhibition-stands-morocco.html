What we produce · Exhibition stands

Exhibition stands, designed and built

Trade fairs, showrooms and brand activations: a stand designed around your brand, then built, installed and taken down by one team.

Talk to us about your event

A stand has a few seconds to stop a visitor walking down an aisle. It has to say who you are from a distance, welcome people comfortably up close, and look as good on the last day of the fair as on the first. We design it around those three moments.

We take care of the design, the construction, the printed surfaces, the lighting, the furniture and the screens, then the installation before the doors open and the dismantling after the last day. One producer follows your stand from the first drawing to the end of the fair.

What we take care of

  • Design and construction
  • Textile and modular stands
  • Backlit walls and light boxes
  • Counters, furniture and screens
  • Installation and dismantling

What we provide

01

Design and construction

Stands made to measure for your brand and for the plan of the hall, from a single counter to an island stand.

02

Textile and modular stands

Tension-fabric (SEG) walls and modular structures: light, quick to set up, and reusable from one fair to the next.

03

Backlit walls and light boxes

Printed fabric lit from behind, for walls and displays that stay bright under the lights of the hall.

04

Counters, furniture and screens

Counters in your colours, high tables, seating, product displays and screens, chosen to fit the stand.

05

Installation and dismantling

Delivery, set-up before the doors open, and dismantling after the last day, anywhere in Morocco.

How a stand comes together

Four steps, with the same producer from start to finish.

  • BriefThe fair, the plan of your space, your products, and what visitors should do on the stand.
  • DesignLayout, materials and visuals, with a clear budget, approved before anything is made.
  • MakeStructure, printing, lighting and furniture prepared and checked before they leave for the venue.
  • Set upInstalled and lit before the opening, then taken down after the last day.
