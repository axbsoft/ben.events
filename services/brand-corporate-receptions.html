Brand & Corporate Receptions

Product launches, inaugurations, open days, press events and networking receptions that bring your brand and your guests together.

Talk to us about your event

A launch, an inauguration or an open day is a brand receiving guests at home. We start from your world to imagine a concept, a set in your colours and a journey that takes each guest from the welcome to the last conversation.

Industrial hall, showroom, reception room or fan zone: we fit the place to the event, with the furniture, the lighting, the screens, the DJ and the entertainment that give it its atmosphere.

What we take care of

  • Concept and theme of the evening
  • Set design in the brand’s colours
  • The guests’ journey, from welcome to goodbye
  • DJ, entertainment and games
  • Furniture, lighting and screens

Frequent questions

Do you organise open days in showrooms and dealerships?

Yes. For CUPRA, we dressed the showroom from morning to evening: the car on a lit stage, totems in the brand’s colours, a welcome bar, a DJ, the lighting of the evening and a game for the visitors.

Can you organise a launch in our own showroom?

Yes. Showroom, industrial hall, reception room or fan zone: we fit the place to the event, with the furniture, the lighting, the screens and the entertainment that give it its atmosphere.

Will the set carry our brand identity?

Yes. We start from your world to imagine a concept and a set in your colours, down to the furniture.

Who takes care of the DJ and the entertainment?

We do. DJ, entertainment and games are part of what we take care of, like the journey of your guests from the welcome to the last conversation.

An event like this in mind?

Give us the date, the place and the number of guests: we will come back with first ideas.
