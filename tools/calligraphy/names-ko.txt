# benevents agency — Korean calligraphy experience: first names whose Korean form is not what
# their letters alone would give.
#
# Korean script writes sounds, so most names need no entry here: the app writes them from the
# way they are spelt in French (Yassine 야신, Fatima Zahra 파티마 자흐라, Achraf 아슈라프).
# This list holds the others: French names with silent letters or nasal vowels (Jean, Vincent),
# English names (John, Michael), and a few established forms (Muhammad 무함마드).
#
# One line per name:   한글[/other accepted form]: spelling spelling spelling
# Spellings are written without accents, in lower case; capitals, accents, hyphens and
# spaces in what the visitor types are ignored when looking a name up.
# The forms follow the spelling rules of the National Institute of Korean Language.
#
# After editing, run:  python3 tools/calligraphy/build_app.py   (see the top of that file)
