# benevents agency — Japanese calligraphy experience: first names whose Japanese form is not what
# their letters alone would give.
#
# Foreign names are written in katakana, sound by sound, so most names need no entry here: the
# app writes them from the way they are spelt in French (Yassine ヤシン, Fatima ファティマ,
# Achraf アシュラフ). This list holds the others: names that take a long vowel (Paul ポール,
# Nour ヌール) or a doubled consonant (Charlotte シャルロット), French names with silent
# letters or nasal vowels (Jean ジャン, Vincent ヴァンサン), and English names (John ジョン).
#
# One line per name:   カタカナ[/other accepted form]: spelling spelling spelling
# Spellings are written without accents, in lower case; capitals, accents, hyphens and
# spaces in what the visitor types are ignored when looking a name up.
#
# After editing, run:  python3 tools/calligraphy/build_app.py   (see the top of that file)
