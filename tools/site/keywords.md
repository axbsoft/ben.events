# One search phrase per page

Each page that search engines list aims at one main phrase. The other words on it only support that phrase.
Before adding a page, check that its phrase is not already taken here: two pages aiming at the same phrase
compete with each other.

No keyword tool was connected when this was written (November 2026). These choices are judgement about what
clients type, not measured search volumes.

The English pages mirror the French ones (event agency in Morocco, event agency in Casablanca, and so on).

## Phrases that get no page of their own

- **organisation événement entreprise Maroc**: the home page. It is what the agency is.
- **organisation cérémonie institutionnelle Maroc**: the ceremonies page.
- **scénographie événementielle, régie technique, sonorisation, éclairage, écran LED**: the audiovisual page.
  One page that covers the whole production is stronger than five short ones that would compete with each other.

## Where the phrase goes on a page

In its title, in its description, and in the text. The large headline of a page can stay a line of the brand:
the small label above it and the title carry the phrase (the home page does this).
